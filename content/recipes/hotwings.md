## Ingredients

### Wings
* 1 lb chicken wings
* 1 tsp salt
* 1 tsp corn starch
* 1 tsp baking powder
* 1 tbsp neutral oil

### Sauce
* 4 tbsp butter
* 2-3 cloves garlic minced fine
* 4 tbsp hot sauce

</div>
<div>

## Directions
### Prep
* Pat wings dry with paper towel.
* Mix dry ingredients in large mixing bowl.
* Toss wings to coat.
* Refrigerate uncovered 4 hours to overnight.

### Air Fry
* Toss wings in oil or spray to coat.
* 250&deg;F 20 min
* Cool 15 minutes
* 400&deg;F 8-10 min

### Sauce
* Mix sauce and melted butter.
* Toss wings to coat.
