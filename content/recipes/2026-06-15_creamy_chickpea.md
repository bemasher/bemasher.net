## Ingredients

|    Amt. | Ingr.                  |
| ------: | :--------------------- |
|  3 tbsp | Extra Virgin Olive Oil |
|    1 ea | Small Onion            |
|    8 ea | Garlic Cloves          |
|  2 tbsp | AP Flour               |
|   1 tsp | Sweet Smoked Paprika   |
|   1 tsp | Dried Oregano          |
|   1 cup | Whole Milk             |
|   1 cup | Broth (Veg, Chx, Bf)   |
|  2 cans | Chickpeas (15.5 oz)    |
| Handful | Chopped Spinach        |
|  2 tbsp | Grated Cheese          |
|   2 tsp | Lemon Juice            |

</div>
<div>

## Instructions

 * Finely chop onion.
 * Roughly chop garlic.
 * Add olive oil to pan over medium heat.
 * Add onion and garlic, stir continuously 3 minutes.
 * Stir in flour, 1-2 minutes.
 * Stir in paprika and oregano.
 * Stir in milk and broth, 4-5 minutes.
 * Drain, rinse, add chickpeas.
 * Season with salt and black pepper.
 * Simmer low heat, 10 minutes.
 * Stir in spinach until lightly wilted.
 * Stir in grated cheese and lemon juice.
