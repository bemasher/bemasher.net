## Foreword
Crispy hot wings, using methods from two internet folks:
* [J Kenji Lopez-Alt's prep/drying.](https://frienji.kenjilopezalt.com/posts/food-lab-recipe-121709152)
* [@LifeByMikeG's air fyer instructions.](https://www.youtube.com/shorts/i0eeJv_eWrk)

## Ingredients

### Wings
* 1 lb chicken wings
* 1 tsp salt
* 1 tsp corn starch
* 1 tsp baking powder

### Sauce
* 4 tbsp butter
* 2-3 cloves garlic minced fine
* 4 tbsp hot sauce

## Directions
### Prep
* Pat wings dry with paper towel.
* Mix dry ingredients in large bowl.
* Toss wings to coat.
* Refrigerate uncovered 4 hours to overnight.

### Cook
* Air fry 250&deg;F 20 min
* Let cool 15 minutes
* Air fry 400&deg;F 8-10 min

### Sauce
* Mix sauce and melted butter.
* Toss wings to coat.
